Pharmacovigilance Interview Questions

Pharmacovigilance interviews commonly assess foundational safety concepts, case-processing logic, documentation quality and the ability to distinguish reported information from medical assessment.

Core concepts to prepare

  • Pharmacovigilance: the science and activities relating to the detection, assessment, understanding and prevention of adverse effects or other medicine-related problems.
  • Adverse event (AE): an untoward medical occurrence in a patient or clinical investigation subject administered a medicinal product, which does not necessarily have a causal relationship with treatment.
  • Adverse drug reaction (ADR): a noxious and unintended response to a medicinal product for which a causal relationship is at least a reasonable possibility, according to the applicable framework.
  • ICSR: an Individual Case Safety Report containing information about one or more suspected adverse reactions in an individual patient at a particular point in time.

Typical ICSR questions

  • What are the minimum criteria for a valid ICSR? Be ready to explain an identifiable patient, identifiable reporter, suspect product and suspected adverse reaction.
  • What is the difference between seriousness and severity? Seriousness is based on regulatory outcome criteria; severity describes the intensity of an event.
  • What is expectedness? Explain how an event is assessed against the applicable reference safety information.
  • What is causality assessment? Describe the evaluation of the relationship between a medicinal product and an event, without treating chronology alone as proof.
  • What are follow-up activities? Explain how missing or clinically relevant information may be requested and documented.

Literature and signal management

  • Explain how literature is searched, screened, deduplicated and assessed for potential reportable cases or safety information.
  • Describe the difference between a safety signal and an established risk.
  • Explain signal validation, prioritization, assessment and recommendation at a high level.
  • Discuss the importance of source traceability, accurate data entry, medical review and quality control.

Interview preparation tips

  • Answer using a clear definition followed by a practical example.
  • Use the terminology and process applicable to the employer’s SOPs and relevant regulations.
  • Do not invent case details. State what information is missing and how it would be handled.
  • Revise current ICH, regional regulatory and company-specific requirements before the interview.
